The trail

    For teachers and parents

    • Runs in the browser. No sign-up, no ads, no downloads. It works on Chromebooks, tablets and phones.
    • Private by design. Drawings and typed words stay on the device. Nothing is uploaded.
    • Sized for a class period. Each station takes 5 to 10 minutes and ends with questions to talk about. Best for ages 7 to 12.
    • Real methods, kid-sized. Every station runs a real technique, small enough to watch: a neural network, trial-and-error learning, a decision tree, a word predictor.
